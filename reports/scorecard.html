01 — Do the percentages mean anything

When it says 60%, does it happen 60% of the time?

Every probability the model ever quoted, pooled and bucketed. A dot on the dashed line means that bucket was exactly right. Dot size is how many predictions sit in it.

This is the honest test. Being right often is easy if you only ever predict the favourite. Being calibrated means the number itself carries information — that a 30% is genuinely rarer than a 60%.

The dots track the line closely, with one consistent bias: at the confident end the model says 78% and reality delivers 74% across 882 predictions. It is slightly too sure of its favourites — which is exactly what the worst misses below look like.
