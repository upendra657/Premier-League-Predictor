1 · The model loses to the market — and that is the point

The bookmaker line is sharper: it has team news, lineups and money flow this model does not. But the model's stated confidence is more trustworthy (ECE 0.0093 vs 0.0104). Discrimination and calibration are different properties, and staking depends on the second one.

Calibration is a targeted remedy, not a free win. Isotonic regression cut Random Forest's calibration error by 56% and its Brier score by 2.4%. It did nothing for XGBoost (+0.3% Brier), because mlogloss is already a strictly proper scoring rule — XGBoost arrives calibrated, so post-hoc fitting only adds fold noise.

This is why naive "bet everything with positive EV" systems lose money — they treat the loss-making tail and the profitable middle as one signal.

Read the third row before you quote the first. The +7.2% uses a selection band chosen with hindsight over the whole sample. Refit that band on 2013/14–2018/19 and apply it blind to the last six seasons, and ROI falls to +1.5% with a confidence interval straddling zero. The honest read: a marginal, unstable edge in a near-efficient market. Quote the strong number, keep this caveat ready.
